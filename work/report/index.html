The board now

Before and after: the stage moves to the top

r36 had the analog stage on the bottom of the new strip (left). r41 has it on the top (right), so every SMD part is on one side and JLC's Economic assembly applies. The op-amp now takes its feedback at the output pin, after R11, so the receiver's input doesn't shift the reading; R9 is 1206; the two M3 holes have no copper under a screw head; the jumpers are labelled TEST and 120R.
