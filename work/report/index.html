The board now

Before and after: the bottom side

The old board held every new part between the module's pin rows (left). The grown board puts the whole analog stage in a new 15 mm strip (right), below the module's PWM/test/supply pins and away from the heater and sensor pins. R11 (470 Ω) now sits between the output clamps and J3-5, so 12 V landing on the output pin can't reach the 5 V rail.
